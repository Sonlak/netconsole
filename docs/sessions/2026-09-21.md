## 21:30 -- fix(fabric): unified port-index so DS-01.bottom renders 4 distinct uplinks

User: "khi tao thêm thiết bị tầng, thì phần topology vẽ lệch, để thêm
thiết bị đấu nối vào thì không phải vẽ trùng link. Có thấy 4 thiết bị
đấu lên ds phải 4 link tách biệt, hình thì đang chỉ thấy 3 link ở ds."

### Root cause

`FabricDiagram.tsx` split port-index assignment between two separate
lists:
- `sourcePortIdx(link, side)` indexed within `linksBySource[link.fromDeviceId].filter(fromSide===side)`
- `targetPortIdx(link, side)` indexed within `linksByTarget[link.toDeviceId].filter(toSide===side)`

A DS port (e.g. `DS-01.bottom`) carries BOTH outgoing trunks (DS→F1/F2/F3,
stored `fromId=DS-01`) AND incoming trunks (F4→DS-01, stored
`fromId=F4-AS-01`). The F1 outgoing link got idx=0 from the source-list
and the F4 incoming link ALSO got idx=0 from the target-list, so both
rendered their DS-01 stub at the same X=432. Lines visually merged at
the stub, and one of the four uplinks appeared missing on screen.

Reproduced on live by dumping 13 `<path d>` strings from the rendered
SVG: both `M 432 552 L 444 748` (F1) and `M 124 748 L 432 552` (F4)
share stubEnd X=432 on DS-01.bottom.

### Fix

Unified per-`(node, side)` incident map. For every (node, side), walk
ALL links incident (outgoing OR incoming), sort by the OTHER endpoint's
`box.x`, assign sequential idx 0..N-1. Both ends of any link now consult
the same map. 4 distinct port positions on DS-01.bottom, 4 visible
trunk lines.

### Files

- `frontend/src/features/fabric/FabricDiagram.tsx` -- dropped
  `linksBySource`/`linksByTarget` partition; new `portIdx` map keyed by
  `(nodeId, side, linkId)`; helper `portIndex(link, nodeId, side)`.
- `AGENTS.md` -- added gotcha #16 documenting the trap.

### Verification

After Deploy #429 (CI #442, Rollback #73 skipped):

DS-01.bottom now has stubs at X = 432, 496, 560, 624.
DS-02.bottom now has stubs at X = 776, 840, 904, 968.

8 trunk paths, 4 per DS, all distinct X. Screenshot confirms 4 separate
lines emerge from each DS to its 4 access switches (F4-AS-01 now
distinct at `ge-0/0/6` leftmost port).

### Lesson

Port-index assignment must be GLOBAL per (node, side), not split by
link direction. The dedup-merged link store has no canonical
"fromId=parent, toId=child" orientation -- the canonical orientation
_after_ merging is determined by `fromIsLex`, which has nothing to do
with the rank direction. So both `DS→access` and `access→DS`-stored
links can land on the same physical port.

If `prisma backend fabricTopology service` is changed to force canonical
orientation, this trap would be impossible at the source. Until then,
the frontend has to dedup-aware its port assignment.

### Result (1166 samples, 180 s, 6.48 req/s aggregate)

  `/api/jobs`) p95 = 400–700 ms under load vs 200–450 ms baseline —
  full JSONB blob scan per call.
